------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 4 - MODELLING AN APPOINTMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

 A newly created appointment prints as:
   08:30  Ava Thompson (mobile 0412 345 678)
          with General Practitioner 1001 - Dr Anna Nguyen
          Consulting room: Room 3 | Accepting new patients: yes

 Appointments order themselves by time, so a mixed collection can be
 sorted before the times have even been put in order:
   Before sorting: 11:00, 08:30, 09:30
   After sorting : 08:30 09:30 11:00
